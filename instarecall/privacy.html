Instarecall Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

Instarecall is an Android app provided by CTF Designs. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information the app stores

Instarecall stores the information needed to create and manage your reminders, including links and text you share to the app, reminder type, due time, notes, and completion status.

Local-only storage

Instarecall stores this information locally on your device. The current version does not require an account, upload your saved items to CTF Designs, synchronize them to a cloud service, or use a CTF Designs backend.

Notifications

If you grant notification permission, Instarecall schedules local notifications on your device for reminders you create. You can disable notifications at any time in Android settings.

Third-party links and apps

Instarecall can receive links or text through Android's sharing features and can open saved links in the appropriate browser or app. Those third-party services operate under their own privacy policies. Instarecall is not affiliated with Instagram, Meta, TikTok, YouTube, Reddit, or other third-party platforms.

Analytics, advertising, and data sales

The current version of Instarecall does not include advertising, analytics SDKs, or tracking SDKs. CTF Designs does not sell or share your Instarecall data.

Permissions

Instarecall may request notification permission so it can alert you when reminders are due. It receives only content you explicitly share to it through Android's sharing interface.

Data retention and deletion

Your saved items remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app's storage, or uninstall Instarecall. Because the current version has no account or backend, CTF Designs does not hold a server-side copy to delete.

Children's privacy

Instarecall is not directed to children under 13, and CTF Designs does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Security

Instarecall relies on Android's application sandbox to protect its local database. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, so you should avoid saving highly sensitive information in reminder notes.

Changes to this policy

If Instarecall later adds accounts, cloud synchronization, analytics, advertising, or other data processing, this policy will be updated before those features are released.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests may be sent to roman@ctfdesigns.com.
